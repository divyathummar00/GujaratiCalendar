# Privacy Policy for Gujarati Calendar

Last updated: October 2026

Gujarati Calendar ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Gujarati Calendar mobile application (the "App") as an ad-supported utility service. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data for anyone who uses our App.

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

#### A. Information Collected by the App Itself
Our App is designed to function locally on your device:
* We do **not** require user accounts or registration.
* We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your name, phone number, email address, contacts, or photos.
* Any user preferences (such as selected district, local festival reminders, or notes) are stored strictly **locally on your device** (via local storage/SQLite) and are never sent to our servers.

#### B. Information Collected by Third-Party Services (Advertising)
To provide free access to our calendar and panchang calculations, the App displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob (Google LLC)**.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, including:
* **Approximate Location:** Derived from IP address for localized ad serving.
* **Device Identifiers:** Such as the Android Advertising ID (GAID) and App Set ID for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, and personalized advertising.
* **App Activity & Interactions:** Ad views, taps, and impressions.
* **Diagnostics & Performance:** SDK performance metrics, load latency, and technical crash data.

For more details on how Google processes your data, please review:
* [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 2. Data Security
The security of your data is important to us. All data transmitted by third-party advertising SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

---

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion
* Any data stored locally by the App is automatically and permanently removed when you uninstall the App or clear app data via your device's settings.
* Third-party data (like Advertising ID and analytics) is managed directly by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device's settings (`Settings > Google > Ads`).

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
